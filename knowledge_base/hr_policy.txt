HUMAN RESOURCE POLICY — Giggs Software Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Company: Giggs Software Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India
Industry: Information Technology / Artificial Intelligence / Software Development

SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION
The HR Policy outlines principles and procedures for managing workforce effectively. It applies to all permanent, probationary, contractual, temporary, and internship employees.

SECTION 2: OBJECTIVES
- Provide clear HR guidelines and procedures
- Ensure equal employment opportunities
- Create a positive and safe workplace
- Establish transparent systems for performance and rewards
- Encourage employee engagement and development
- Maintain discipline and professional ethics
- Comply with applicable employment laws

SECTION 3: RECRUITMENT & SELECTION
Merit-based recruitment process including: Resume Screening, Technical Assessment, HR Interview, Technical/Managerial Interview, Final Selection Offer Rollout.
Required documents: Government ID, Address Proof, Educational Certificates, Experience Certificates, Passport Photos, PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Bank Details.

SECTION 4: EMPLOYMENT CATEGORIES
- Permanent Employees
- Probationary Employees
- Contract Employees
- Interns/Trainees
- Consultants/Freelancers
Terms and conditions differ by employment type.

SECTION 5: PROBATION & CONFIRMATION
Probation period: 3 to 6 months for all newly hired employees.
During probation: performance and conduct evaluated, employment may be terminated without notice for unsatisfactory performance or misconduct.
Confirmation provided in writing after successful probation. Probation may be extended if required.

SECTION 6: CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees must: treat colleagues respectfully, maintain confidentiality, avoid harassment or discrimination, follow ethical practices, use company resources responsibly, maintain punctuality.
Strictly prohibited: harassment or bullying, fraud or theft, unauthorized disclosure of confidential information, substance abuse during working hours, violence or abusive behavior, misuse of company systems.

SECTION 7: WORKING HOURS & ATTENDANCE
Working Days: Monday to Friday
Office Timing: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM
Lunch Break: 1 Hour
Employees must mark attendance through designated systems, inform managers of late arrival or absence, maintain regular attendance and punctuality.
Repeated late attendance or absenteeism may lead to disciplinary action.

SECTION 8: LEAVE POLICY
Leave Entitlement:
- Casual Leave (CL): 12 days annually
- Sick Leave (SL): 12 days annually
- Earned Leave (EL): 15 days annually
- Public Holidays: As per company calendar
- Maternity Leave: As per applicable law (26 weeks for first two children as per Maternity Benefit Act)
- Paternity Leave: 5 working days
Leave Rules: Leave must be applied through official system. Emergency leave should be informed immediately. Unauthorized absence may result in salary deductions. Leave encashment follows company policy.

SECTION 9: COMPENSATION & PAYROLL
Salary Components: Basic Salary, House Rent Allowance, Special Allowance, Performance Incentives, Bonus where applicable.
Payroll Process: Salaries credited monthly. Statutory deductions include PF, ESI, TDS. Payslips shared electronically.

SECTION 10: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT & APPRAISAL
Assessment parameters: Quality of work, Technical competency, Communication skills, Team collaboration, Timely delivery, Innovation and initiative.
Appraisals conducted annually or bi-annually.
Performance outcomes impact: Salary increments, Promotions, Bonuses, Training opportunities.

SECTION 11: TRAINING & DEVELOPMENT
Employees may receive: Technical training, Soft skills training, Leadership development, AI/ML and software development workshops, Certification support programs.

SECTION 12: WORK FROM HOME POLICY
Remote work allowed based on role requirements and management approval.
WFH requirements: available during working hours, maintain productivity and communication, ensure confidentiality and data security, attend virtual meetings regularly.
Company reserves the right to modify remote work permissions.

SECTION 13: DRESS CODE
Acceptable: Smart casuals, Business formals during meetings and client interactions.
Discouraged: Offensive clothing, Excessively casual attire, Clothing displaying offensive messages.

SECTION 14: EMPLOYEE BENEFITS
Eligible employees may receive: Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), Health Insurance, Paid Leaves, Performance Incentives, Learning and Development Opportunities, Flexible Work Arrangements.
Benefits vary by employee category.

SECTION 15: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
Employees may raise concerns about: work environment, harassment, discrimination, compensation, team conflicts, managerial concerns.
Process: Discuss with Reporting Manager → Escalate to HR if unresolved → Formal review by management → Resolution and communication.
All grievancdled confidentially.

SECTION 16: PREVENTION OF SEXUAL HARASSMENT (POSH)
Zero tolerance policy. Sexual harassment includes: unwelcome physical contact, sexually colored remarks, inappropriate messages, demands for sexual favors, offensive jokes.
Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) established as per law. All complaints investigated confidentially.

SECTION 17: DISCIPLINARY POLICY
Disciplinary action for: misconduct, breach of confidentiality, fraudulent activities, policy violations, repeated absenteeism, harassment or abusive behavior.
Actions include: Warning Letter, Salary deduction, Suspension, Termination.

SECTION 18: DATA SECURITY & CONFIDENTIALITY
Confidential information includes: source code, client information, financial records, internal documents, login credentials, product strategies.
Employees must not: share confidential data externally, use unauthorized software, access restricted systems without permission.

SECTION 19: RESIGNATION & TERMINATION
Resignation: Written resignation required.
Notice period: Probationary employees 15 days, Confirmed employees 30 to 60 days.
Termination for: poor performance, misconduct, policy violations, business restructuring.
Exit formalities must be completed before final settlement.

SECTION 20: TRAVEL & REIMBURSEMENT
Reimbursable expenses for official travel: travel expenses, accommodation, meals, local transportation.
All reimbursements must have bills and manager approval.

SECTION 21: HEALTH, SAFETY & WORKPLACE CONDUCT
Employees must follow safety procedures, maintain workplace cleanliness, avoid unsafe practices, report hazards immediately.
Prohibited on premises: smoking, alcohol consumption, illegal substances.

SECTION 22: EQUAL OPPORTUNITY
Equal opportunity employer. No discrimination based on: gender, religion, caste, race, disability, age, sexual orientation, marital status.
Employment decisions based solely on merit and qualifications.

SECTION 23: WHISTLEBLOWER POLICY
Employees encouraged to report unethical behavior, fraud, corruption, policy violations.
Reports handled confidentially, investigated fairly, protected from retaliation.
Report to HR or senior management.

SECTION 24: AMENDMENTS
Company reserves right to amend, modify, or discontinue any part of this policy.
Employees informed of major changes through official communication channels.
