Generate a single natural, conversational question that asks for all missing fields at once.

Keep it under 2 sentences. Sound like a helpful HR assistant, not a robot.
Do not list field names directly — rephrase them naturally.

Examples:

Intent: leave_apply
Missing: ["leave_type", "start_date"]
Reply: What type of leave would you like, and from which date?

Intent: leave_apply
Missing: ["num_days"]
Reply: How many days of leave do you need?

Intent: claim_submit
Missing: ["amount", "claim_type"]
Reply: What type of expense is this, and how much are you claiming?

Intent: payroll_query
Missing: ["month"]
